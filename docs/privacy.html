拍照學單字｜隱私權政策

生效日：2026-10-18。本政策說明「拍照學單字」如何處理資料。分享使用統計預設是關閉的。

使用統計

打開 App 裡的「設定 → 分享使用統計」之後，操作紀錄會送到 PostHog（主機 us.i.posthog.com）。送出的只有：這次安裝產生的隨機代號、開啟 App、所在分頁、學習語言、母語、程度與程度來源、有沒有加入照片或單字（不含照片和單字本身）、複習開始與結束，以及單次作答的評分、題型、對錯、提示次數、作答時間與下次間隔天數。不送姓名、帳號、電子郵件或廣告識別碼。隨時可以在設定裡關掉；還沒送出的紀錄會一併捨棄。

同一個開關也控制當機回報：打開後，App 當機或發生未處理的錯誤時，會把錯誤類型、程式呼叫堆疊、App 版本與 build 號、作業系統版本與裝置型號送到 Sentry。不送照片、單字內容、電子郵件、使用者名稱或操作軌跡，也不含錯誤訊息文字。關閉時不會送出。

照片與辨識

照片和字卡保存在這台裝置（登入帳號並開啟同步時，另存一份在你的帳號下）。使用辨識時，照片會傳到你設定的辨識伺服器，只在記憶體裡即時辨識，不會存成照片檔。伺服器可能留下辨識結果的文字快取（單字與分數，不含照片），以免同一張照片重複運算。照片不會送到分析服務。

帳號與分享

不登入也能使用 App、下載公開的卡片集。登入是選用的：用來把資料備份到雲端、換手機還原，以及「分享」自己的集合（Sign in with Apple、Google，或 Email＋密碼）。登入會收集：暱稱、母語、程度，以及 Apple／Google 提供的使用者代號與 Email，或你填的 Email；雲端備份與自動同步的資料（單字、相片冊、照片、複習紀錄、設定）存放在你的帳號下，刪除帳號時一併刪除；登入後資料會自動同步到你用同一帳號登入的其他裝置，可在「設定 → 資料」關閉同步，或只關閉照片同步；密碼只存加鹽雜湊，登入憑證存在 iOS 鑰匙圈。你分享的單字集或圖片集（照片、單字、程度標籤、名稱與簡介）會公開在平台上，其他使用者可以瀏覽與下載，並顯示你的暱稱。照片上傳前請確認沒有他人的臉或隱私資訊；照片會先通過自動安全檢查才會上架。你可以隨時撤回分享、在 App 內刪除帳號（帳號與你分享的內容會一併刪除）；其他使用者可以檢舉不當集合或封鎖作者，我們會下架違規內容並停用帳號。分享統計（人數、下載次數）只計次，不含個人行為追蹤。

登入後的使用數字：只有登入雲端帳號時，App 才會每天最多一次把下列「數字」傳到我們的伺服器：已下載的相片冊名稱、複習過的卡片數（累計與近 7 天）、照片數、打開照片的次數、最後使用時間與 App 版本。不傳照片內容、單字內容或位置；沒登入就完全不傳。用途是了解 App 有沒有人在用、哪些相片冊受歡迎，只有管理者看得到，與你的 Email 對應。刪除帳號時這些數字會一併刪除；網路不通時 App 照常離線使用，下次再補傳。

不做跨 App 追蹤

本 App 只做自己的使用統計，不把資料拿去跟其他 App 或網站比對，也不使用廣告識別碼，所以不會跳出「允許追蹤」的詢問，也不需要 App Tracking Transparency。

Privacy policy (English)

Effective 2026-10-18. Sharing usage statistics is off unless you turn it on in Settings.

When it is on, activity goes to PostHog at us.i.posthog.com: a random id for this install, app opens, the current tab, learning language, native language, level and how it was chosen, whether a photo or word was added (not the photo or the word), review start and finish, and for each answer the rating, exercise type, right or wrong, hint count, time spent and the next interval in days. No name, account, email or advertising identifier. Turning the switch off discards anything not yet sent.

The same switch controls crash reports: when it is on, a crash or unhandled error sends the error type, stack trace, app version and build number, OS version and device model to Sentry. It does not include photos, word content, email, user name, activity trail or the error message text. Nothing is sent while it is off.

Photos and cards stay on the device (with a copy under your account if you sign in and sync is on). For recognition, the photo is sent to the server you configured, processed in memory, and not saved as a photo file. That server may keep a text cache of the result (words and scores, not the photo). Photos are not sent to analytics.

Accounts and sharing: you can use the app and download public decks without signing in. Signing in (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password) is optional: it backs your data up to the cloud for a new phone, and lets you share your own deck. Cloud backup and automatic sync data (words, albums, photos, review history, settings) is stored under your account and deleted with it. Once signed in, your data syncs to other devices using the same account; you can turn sync, or just photo sync, off in Settings → Data. We collect a nickname, native language and level, plus the Apple or Google user id and email, or the email you give; passwords are stored only as salted hashes and the login token lives in the iOS Keychain. A deck you share (photos, words, level tags, name, description) is public on the platform with your nickname, and other users can browse and download it. Make sure photos contain no one else’s face or private information; photos pass an automatic safety check before they go live. You can withdraw a share or delete your account in the app at any time (the account and everything you shared are deleted). Users can report a deck or block an author; we remove violating content and may disable accounts. Share counts and download counts are plain tallies, not behavioural tracking.

Signed-in usage numbers: only while you are signed in to a cloud account, the app sends at most once a day to our server: the names of the photo albums you downloaded, how many cards you reviewed (total and last 7 days), your photo count, how many times you opened a photo, the last time you used the app and the app version. No photo content, word content or location is sent, and nothing is sent when signed out. It is used to see whether the app is used and which albums are popular; only the administrator can see it, linked to your email. These numbers are deleted with your account; offline, the app works as usual and sends later.

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier, so it does not ask for permission to track.
